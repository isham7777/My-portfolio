How it works

Grounded in your campus, not the open web

Every answer traces back to a document, calendar, or system your university already maintains — so it stays right when policies change mid-semester.

a.

It reads what your offices publish

Registrar bulletins, dining menus, library hours, housing contracts, advising sheets — indexed directly from the offices that own them, refreshed as they change.

b.

It checks against your own record

With your sign-in, it can see your enrollment, holds, and aid status — so an answer about "your" deadline is actually about your deadline, not the general catalog.

c.

It tells you where an answer came from

Every response names the office or document behind it, so you can go straight to the source — or the right human — when a question needs a real person.

d.

It hands off cleanly when it should

Appeals, accommodations, and anything requiring judgment get routed to the right office with your context attached, instead of a dead-end chatbot loop.
